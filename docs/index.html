01 / THE IDEA

Student outcomes set direction.
Joint outcomes select support.

On-policy distillation trains a student on its own responses using a stronger teacher’s token-level feedback. But that feedback does not always agree with correctness. DuoOPD verifies both models: the student’s outcome determines whether to reinforce or suppress its response, while the pair’s outcomes determine the teacher’s scoring context and how weights are allocated.

Explore the four outcomes below. The teacher is frozen; the student learns from freshly generated responses.

Reinforce shared successTeacher ✓   Student ✓

Reinforce every valid token of the correct student response. Teacher preferences in the original context determine the strength of positive feedback, using Softplus.

Ground correction in teacher successTeacher ✓   Student ✗

Give the teacher its verified answer as additional scoring context. It then assigns negative feedback to the student’s failed response. Only the teacher sees the reference.

Preserve student-led successTeacher ✗   Student ✓

Reinforce the whole correct response with one positive weight shared among student-only successes within the same task and rollout batch, preserving the task’s mean reinforcement scale.

Discourage shared failureTeacher ✗   Student ✗

Suppress every valid token of the failed student response. Teacher preferences adjust the strength of suppression under the original context; verification fixes its direction.

02 / THE EVIDENCE

Gains across model families and tasks.

DuoOPD achieves the highest mean macro accuracy among the six distillation methods for both model families and both additional task mixtures.
